Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information Rowbrawl ("the app", "we") handles. The app has no user accounts and never asks for your name, e-mail address or contact details.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, trophies, skills, settings and statistics) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. You can also erase it in Settings › Reset progress.

2. Online duels

To match you with other players, the app signs in anonymously to Google Firebase. Firebase creates a random identifier that is not linked to your identity. During matchmaking and a match we store your in-game nickname (picked at random by the app), trophy count, block colour, board state, score and match events. This data is used only to run the match. Your queue entry is deleted when the match starts and the match record is deleted shortly after it ends. If a match is interrupted (for example the app is closed), its record may remain on the server until it is removed during routine maintenance.

3. Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location, device information and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent with Google's consent form; you can change your choice any time in Settings › Ad privacy options. On iOS, personalised ads are shown only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details; we only receive a confirmation of what you bought so we can deliver it.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Your choices and rights

You can reset ad consent, deny tracking, delete your local progress or uninstall the app at any time. To request deletion of any online data linked to your device, contact us at the address below; because the identifier is anonymous, please send it from the device or describe when you last played.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
